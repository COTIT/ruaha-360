Rules this design enforces visually

Capacity carries a hatched Basis: planned pill inside the figure's own cell.

Prospective and approved sit in two framed cells with a rule between them — no layout can sum them.

Every estimate has a hatched ground and an ESTIMATE tag.

Every price has an Indicative pill, not a grey parenthesis.

The not-a-sale sentence is 14px body on a bordered panel — it cannot shrink into fine print.

Planted area keeps its "not land area" note on every surface it appears.

Empty is sunken sand with a dashed mark; error is a red left rule with role="alert".

Secondary text is --ink-2 (6.6:1); notes are --ink-3 (4.8:1). Nothing below 12px ships.

Out of scope, and untouched: no new screens, routes, fields or controls; no dark palette; no new dependency — every mark, meter and hatch here is CSS; no change to what any figure means; no invented Kiswahili. Every data-testid shown sits on an element that still means the same thing.

For implementation: take the tokens and scales from Foundations into src/styles/globals.css first — three inks, three surfaces, one hatch, six spacing steps, eight type steps — then apply them screen by screen in the order numbered here. pnpm e2e is the check that nothing moved: every selector in §8 of the handoff is preserved above.
